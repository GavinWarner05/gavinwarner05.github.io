Method & limitations

A baseline to test, not proven accuracy.

Probabilities use median two-sided proportional vig removal from an odds-consensus snapshot at approximately 15:25–15:27 UTC on October 25. This is an untrained baseline, not evidence of forecasting skill. The diagnostic portfolio tests every selected winner, even without a positive price edge. The original value portfolio used a ≥3-percentage-point difference from the quoted break-even probability.

All figures are hypothetical. Fees and minimums are not fully verified; Real warns of 5% maximum slippage. Quotes can change, and there is no guarantee of profit or fills. Started games are unavailable to the calculator; the frozen pregame ledger is retained. No outcomes or scores are inferred. No actual-spending mode, purchases, bidding or account connection are provided. NFL predictions are excluded.
